# Cookie Policy

*Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Zedda Labs*

## 1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and browser `localStorage` are small text files or key-value entries stored in your web browser by websites you visit. They are commonly used to make websites work efficiently and remember user preferences across sessions.

---

## 2. How Zedda Uses Preference Storage

Zedda documentation does **not** use intrusive tracking cookies. We utilize standard, non-sensitive browser `localStorage` solely for functional preferences:

1. **`zedda-theme`**: Remembers your preferred appearance mode (`dark` or `light`) so the site renders with your desired theme without flashing.
2. **`zedda-sidebar-scroll`**: Preserves your sidebar scroll position when navigating between documentation guides and API references.

---

## 3. No Analytics or Advertising Cookies

- **No Third-Party Ad Trackers:** We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies.
- **No Intrusive Analytics:** We do not track individual browsing profiles or share data with data brokers.

---

## 4. Managing Your Preferences

You can clear or disable `localStorage` and cookies at any time through your browser's settings:

- **Chrome / Edge / Firefox / Safari:** Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Browsing Data / Site Data.
